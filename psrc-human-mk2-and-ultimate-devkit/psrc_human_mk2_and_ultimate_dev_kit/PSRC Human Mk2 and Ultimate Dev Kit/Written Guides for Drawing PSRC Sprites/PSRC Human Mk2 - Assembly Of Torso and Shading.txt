PSRC Human Mk2 - Assembly Of Torso and Shading

--

When assembling torsos - for all genders, all ages, muscles/casual and all body types - this is the process:

* Shoulders, bottoms, necks, tits/moobs, bellies are prefabs.

* Once prefabs are assembled, the connecting sectors are shaded shut between the components.

* For muscular bodies, they combine the muscle belly package + ribbed/bumped torso side shading.
* For average/smooth bodies, they simply are smooth.

--

Shading style of Normal - Muscular - Clothed version of Arms, Legs and Torso:
.Normal - average smooth shading - vastly differing silhouettes between SML sizes.
.Muscular - shiny tight shading with sectors - silhouettes only differ slightly between SML sizes.
.Clothed - flat matte shading with barely any highlights - silhouettes are combined max of Normal/Muscular.

*SML - Small, Medium, Large.

--

Torso Belly Sector - Muscles

TYPE 1 - Provides differentiation to muscle strenght based on shoulder size - i.e bigger = better.
Lean Muscles uses 3 sprites depending on should size. (Small - small muscles, Medium - full pack, Large - mega pack.)
