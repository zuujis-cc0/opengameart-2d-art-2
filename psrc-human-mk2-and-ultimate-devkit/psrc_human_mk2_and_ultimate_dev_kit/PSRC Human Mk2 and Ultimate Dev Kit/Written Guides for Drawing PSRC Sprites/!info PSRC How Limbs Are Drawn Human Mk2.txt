PSRC Human mk2 Character Information
====================================

How limbs are drawn:

--

Arms - (Sideview)
* simplest to draw.
* emphasize shoulder.

Hands - (Topview / Exterior)
* easiest to draw.
* best for holding objects.
* excellent for hand gestures.

Legs - (Pseudo Front-Side view)
* pure sideview legs lack detail
  so they're not enough.
* legs are drawn first front, then
  extruded backwards to create side.
* this way knees are properly visible,
  it matches with torso's structure,
  and more detail for limb and cloth
  is now possible.

Feet - (Sideview)
* simplest to draw.
* shoes are easiest cloth to draw.  

Torso - (Complex front-side view)
* initially carved out into a desired-ish
  silhouette of a 26x16px rectangle chunk.
* then partitioned into zones, namely
  shoulders, bottom, belly and breasts,
  with neck and waist coming in later
  adapting to the rest of the zones.
  all mainly for clarity purposes.
* sort of emphasize the same "draw
  front first, then extrude to side"
  method, but unlike legs, there is no
  strict symmetry for the front part,
  making the torso's structure more 
  organic than rest of the limbs.
  
--
  
For all limbs, extensive reference images were
used to get measurements, proportions and
shapes somewhat correct, within the confines
of pixel art, taking liberties where deemed
necessary for sake of simplicity or readability.
Hence why the head is slightly larger than a
real-life equivalent or why the torso's limb
connections are perhaps a bit odd looking.

I'm aiming at a slightly cartoony look in PSRC
that would work better with a 2D physics engine,
since collisions work more reliably if objects
are not too thin. I also think it looks cuter, more
endearing and clear. The PSRC sprites are intended for
game projects primarily, hence the priorities
that shaped the design decisions of how it looks.
