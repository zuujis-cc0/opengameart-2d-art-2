Layering the human correctly:
=============================

Whether you're a gamedev or illustrator and need info on how to layer the PSRC human correctly in your editor, this will help understand the order.
-XX numbers are negative, so they go BEHIND thing, +XX numbers are positive, so they go FRONT of thing. +1 is the torso, the central CORE piece.

Because the PSRC human is modular, clothes are seperate sprites, hence why some layers are reserved for them to avoid visibility conflicts.

--
