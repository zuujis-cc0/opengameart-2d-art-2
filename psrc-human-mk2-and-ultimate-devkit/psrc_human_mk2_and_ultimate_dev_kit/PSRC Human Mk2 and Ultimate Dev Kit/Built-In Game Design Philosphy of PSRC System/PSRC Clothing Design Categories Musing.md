# Clothing Design Categories 
(PSRC Design Musings)

## Categories/Levels:
1. Vanity, fashion, fetish, joke, advert, costume, naked, domestic, indoor, lounge, recreation, socialising, official.
Inert clean dry windless 22'c environment.
Clothes are optional, may as well be naked as they don't truly offer any protection - they're more meant for visual appeal only or for purposes of modesty or representation.
Includes underwear, shirts, pants, dresses, lesser shoes (not trekking goretex type of stuff, that belongs to category 2), business suits, fast fashion clothing, etc.

2. Basic, mundane, practical, light protection against elements, weather, outdoor, everyday wear, dirt, dust, grime, wet resistant, somewhat durable and flexible.
Usable anywhere indoor or outdoor, has variants for temperatures between -0'c to +50'c. Used also often as under layer for heavier clothes. Can be intended for work, casual, military, science, exploration, piloting, driving. Basic jackets, military fatigues, trekker clothes, hunter clothes, 

3. Extreme conditions general use heavy protective, clothes, armor and gear. Anything that mundane/basic clothes alone are not enough to handle. Extra durable and advanced.
Considered overkill or inappropriate for casual/mundane settings yet not specialized, can be used in a variety of situation. Heavy combat armor, arctic jackets, motorcycle impact armor suit, ghillie stealth suit, mecha pilot suit, hypersonic fighter pilot suit, etc.

4. Specialised purpose tactical clothes, armor and protective equipment against very specific narrow hazards that are not common to encounter otherwise.
Not used anywhere outside of context. Stuff like deep diving suits, hazard environment suits, power armor, exo-suit, exo-skeleton, steel-worker or lava resistant clothing, spacesuits, neuralsuits, plugsuits, etc.

NOTE: elements of fashion and vanity *can* be present for categories 2 3 4, but in a way that never violates the function or effectiveness of the original cloth in any capacity.

## Negative things to avoid: 
-high visibility must not be lost by fashion that decreases or confuses vision.
-mobility must not be lost due to fashion elements attached to clothes, causing awkward limits.
-camo must not compromised by hi-vis fashion elements.
-protective features of clothes not must not be ruined or complemented by fashion mods, that themselves do not either improve/enhance protection or make no sense given the context. (i.e. feather flaps on a firesuit or paper ribbons on a swimsuit)

## Positive things to do:
-colors, textures, patterns, ornaments that either complement or mix well with the cloth.
-additional doodads, greebles, extensions, hairs, ribbons, spikes, spokes, patches, feathers and other 3D elements that complement or mix well with the cloth.
